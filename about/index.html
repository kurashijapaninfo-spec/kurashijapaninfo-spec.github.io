About Kurashi Japan

Kurashi Japan publishes easy-to-read guides about daily life, work and official procedures in Japan for foreign residents, in Vietnamese, Indonesian and Nepali. Every article is based on information from Japanese government and public bodies, and lists its official sources. Articles are drafted with AI assistance and checked against those sources before publishing, and they are reviewed regularly.

当サイトは、在日外国人向けに日本の生活・仕事・行政手続きを母国語で分かりやすく解説する情報サイトです。記事は公的機関の情報を根拠にAIの支援を受けて作成し、出典を明記しています。
